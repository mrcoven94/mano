# Decisions

Settled on October 26, 2026. Change one only with David, and record the change here with the date.

## Product

1. **Promise:** apply once, for anything that makes your life better. Benefits, cash assistance, jobs, scholarships and training, plus any form a person hands to Mano.
2. **Who gets an agent:** every resident, every agency staff member, every nonprofit case manager. Same code, three role packs (skills, tools, permissions). An organization has an org agent whose policies and programs its staff agents inherit.
3. **Consent:** a resident shares a case only by explicit grant, to a specific organization, for a specific purpose. Either side can request documents. The resident can revoke future access at any time (revoking cannot recall what was already sent, and Mano says so).
4. **Authorized representatives:** consent in Mano is not legal representative status. Where a program requires a designation, the resident signs the program's own form; the resident attests, Mano assists.
5. **Office profiles, not office agents:** every government office has a labeled, independent profile from day one. A profile cannot converse, request records or send notices. When an office claims its profile, it becomes an org agent with staff seats.
6. **Honesty:** Mano never presents itself as a human or as the person. See AGENTS.md rule 3.
7. **Price:** free forever for applying, renewals, deadlines, reminders and filing; nothing that prevents someone losing help is paid. Plus is $8 a month or $60 a year for things that cost us money (calls on your behalf, mailed and faxed letters, a priority line). Organizations pay per staff seat.
8. **Partner extensions:** outside services plug in as extensions (tools, a skill, the exact data they ask for, who pays). The agent offers one only when it helps; the resident approves each one and can remove it. We never sell personal data.
9. **An app and a platform** (Oct 26): residents use a React Native app (Expo) that builds iOS, Android and the web from one codebase. Staff and case managers work on the web. Mano is also a platform: a public API, an SDK, the open protocol and the extension manifest let other organizations and developers build on it, and anyone can deploy their own copy.

## Hackathon scope (spine first)

The spine must run end to end from October 30 and every day after:

1. Maria photographs or films her mail; Mano splits it into letters, reads each one and builds her deadlines.
2. Mano fills out her renewal, she approves the exact version, and it is submitted to the sandbox agency.
3. The county caseworker's agent (an org that claimed its profile) asks for a missing pay stub; Maria answers with a photo.
4. The caseworker approves; the org agent enters the case into a mock legacy system by computer use; a notice goes out.
5. On camera: a document changes after approval and the stale approval is blocked; a submission times out and is reconciled without a duplicate; a planted scam link is caught.

Then, in order, while the spine stays green: phone and hold-for-me on our own sandbox line, iMessage and SMS, any-form filling on unseen forms, one program built from one real policy document and reviewed by a person, one partner extension on a public API, jobs and scholarships. The resident app ships on Android and the web for the hackathon; iOS goes to TestFlight only if Apple enrollment clears in time. Out of scope for the hackathon: payments, nationwide form learning.

## Proof

A held-out test, frozen before any tuning: renewal and document scenarios with unreadable pages, missing evidence, conflicting dates, planted instructions and scam links. Mano is compared with a plain reminder tool and a general agent with the same tools. Results report correct packets, missed deadlines, blocked unsafe actions, failures, cost and time. No claims about dollars saved or losses prevented. A real caseworker reviews sample packets on camera.

## Platform

- Engine on Cloudflare (per-person Durable Objects with their own SQLite, R2 for files, Browser Run, Sandbox, Email Routing, AI Gateway). Chosen over Vercel because calls on hold need hour-long connections, idle agents should cost nothing at 10 million people, and per-person databases isolate data by construction.
- Shared relational data in PlanetScale Postgres through Hyperdrive.
- Agent computers on Modal Sandboxes (sponsor credits, per-second billing, the same platform as the Glimmer guardian); Cloudflare Browser Run for simple browsing and login handoff. Muse Spark 1.3 drives the computer (Wonder's computer tool was not enabled on our key on Oct 26).
- Website and staff screens on Vercel (`apps/site`, Next.js). Resident app in `apps/app` (React Native with Expo; Android builds through EAS need no Apple account).
- Model calls go through Cloudflare AI Gateway once CF_ACCOUNT_ID and AI_GATEWAY_ID are set; until then the client calls api.meta.ai directly (Oct 26 smoke run confirmed the direct path with a synthetic prompt).

## Changes

- **October 29, 2026, how Mano is built.** One Claude Code session leads and builds through one builder agent per workstream; GPT Sol reviews every pull request; the lead merges with merge commits after the merge gate in `AGENTS.md`. The separate architect thread and the Meter workstream (LAP-602) are retired. Commits carry no AI co-author trailers.
- **October 29, 2026, the spine.** The spine in `AGENTS.md` (Maria texts, sends a photo of her letter, gets her deadline and estimate, approves, and Mano files her renewal in the sandbox portal after Guardian checks it) replaces the Hackathon scope list above. Texting is now the front door, not a later step. The three safety cases (stale approval blocked, timeout reconciled without a duplicate, scam link caught) stay required. The caseworker, missing pay stub and mock legacy entry steps move to the backlog (Orgs, Spine, Screens).
- **October 29, 2026, Meta models only at runtime.** Following the hackathon's ruling, every model Mano calls at runtime is a Meta model, including on-device ones. Speech output on calls (previously Deepgram) is dropped: Mano does not speak on phone calls; fax stays. The browser's built-in `speechSynthesis` is allowed, per the organizers' ruling on October 28, 2026.
- **October 29, 2026, Guardian cold start.** Guardian scales to zero by default (`MANO_GUARDIAN_MIN_CONTAINERS` is 0), and Mano warms it when a conversation starts. The `guardian-modal` workflow's `min_containers` input is set to 1 only while the demo is recorded or judges are testing, then set back to 0.
- **October 29, 2026, the agent brain on Eve.** One Eve agent in `apps/agent` interprets each text, remembers through Engine, proposes actions and keeps reminders, reached through one synchronous turn route. Engine is the system of record and the only approval authority: the agent proposes actions to Engine's `/action`, shows the frozen approval and answers it with the person's yes. Channels stays the messaging transport (webhooks, consent, STOP, bubbles, spend) and falls back to its plain model answer when the agent is unreachable. Muse is called directly as an AI SDK model, never through a gateway string.
- **October 30, 2026, deadlines Mano tracks itself.** A deadline from a letter, a case or the person is stored on the person's agent in Engine, one per idempotency key, and Mano texts it on its own 14, 7 and 2 days before and on the day, at 10:00 in the zone of the person's state (Pacific when unknown). A deadline learned late gets one nudge at the next 10:00 unless a planned one is under 3 days away. Nudges are reminder rows, so they share the reminder delivery path (STOP, the conversation generation, the per-key dedupe), and they only go between 9:00 and 20:00 local on their own day; a nudge that misses its day is dropped rather than sent with a wrong count. Before every handoff, retries included, a nudge whose deadline closed, moved or was deleted is withdrawn. A nudge never carries a letter's own words: it names the action only from an explicit kind (renewal, recertification, interview, hearing, appeal, payment, documents) and the program only when it is a known one ("Your CalFresh renewal is due"); with no kind it names no action ("Your CalFresh deadline is in 12 days", or "the deadline from the letter you sent on" the day it arrived). The title is kept only for the agent, quoted as data. Every caller must send the conversation generation it prepared a deadline under. A nudge only offers the next step; acting still needs an approval. Deleting the conversation deletes the deadlines too, since they hold sentences from the person's letters.
- **October 30, 2026, the desktop never files.** The hosted desktop (`packages/computer`) fills a form and stops; filing happens only through Engine's action gateway after the person's yes, with the portal token the desktop never holds. The browser-filing executor is deleted. The computer-use loop never acknowledges a safety check: a pending check ends the run and Engine decides. There is no live view (VNC is removed), so a login prompt ends the run. Each desktop has a per-session secret on every control route, mounts only its own person's profile volume (named by an HMAC of the Engine person id), and reaches only the configured portal host, enforced by Modal's outbound domain allowlist and a Chromium URL policy.
- **November 2, 2026, quality CI when the model provider is down.** At David's request, after a billing outage at the model provider kept the quality smoke red for three days: a check call answered with HTTP 402 makes the smoke and the nightly report "model provider unavailable" (a neutral check, a green job, the outage named in the job summary) instead of failing. Only 402 counts; 401, 429 and 5xx keep failing as before, because a bad key or our own config can cause them. A `smoke-replay` job runs the smoke's text line and safety checks on recorded synthetic replies on every pull request, with no key. Neither check is made required.
- **November 2, 2026, the person's document list.** Engine records each document it stores for a person: when it arrived, the media type read from its bytes, its size, what Mail found it to be (a letter, whether or not Mail saved a deadline from it) and the conversation generation. The agent lists the current conversation's documents, newest first, each named in plain words by the day the person sent it in their own time zone ("the letter you sent on November 2"). Each listed document says whether the executor Engine is configured with could file it, by the same media type and size check the sandbox executor applies before filing (SANDBOX_MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES, 5 MiB by default; Engine itself stores up to 10 MB); simulated mode judges by the same limits, so it picks what a sandbox deploy would. A document stored before sizes were recorded counts as not fileable rather than reading its bytes back from R2 to measure it. A renewal attaches the document a profile value was read from if it is fileable, else the newest fileable one, and Mano asks for one only when none on file is, saying plainly whether the newest is too large or the wrong kind of file. The approval question names the attached document by that label, frozen into the draft, so the person knows what their yes sends. Deleting the conversation removes its documents from the list.
- **November 3, 2026, Guardian sees each action unredacted.** At David's request, after three review rounds on PR 108 kept finding the same thing: every value removed before Guardian is a place an attack can hide, and pattern matching cannot make removal both thorough and safe (a pattern that removes enough also erases an instruction written to look like a name, a street or a case number, and one that keeps every instruction misses values written a new way). Guardian receives each action as Mano would send it: the type, the recipient, the purpose, the payload with every field and its real value (a submit's name, birth date, address, phone, household size and income, and any text or claims), the document hash when a document is attached, every cited source with its quote, the consents on record, and whether the person or content asked. Nothing is removed or relabeled, and Engine no longer reads the profile at check time. Guardian is Meta's open-weight Muse Glimmer on our own Modal GPU. Our code stores nothing and logs no request or answer body (`packages/guardian/modal/app.py` has no prints of either, vLLM's offline engine there has no request logger, and errors name what failed, never the text). Modal kept the web function's inputs and outputs for up to 7 days until the entry below moved Guardian to a Modal Server, which Modal does not store. No rule forbids Guardian seeing the action, since it is the same content the person approves and the recipient receives. A personal form field that holds an email address or a link is still held before Guardian is asked, with a plain reason, because no real name, birth date, address or phone holds one; Guardian reading every field is the defense. Guardian's prompt treats the person's later yes as consent for the action's own recipient, needs no cited source for form answers, checks that the form fits its purpose and its numbers could be true, and blocks any other destination, an email address or a link that is not the recipient or consented, or an instruction in any field. This supersedes the redaction approach of PR 104 and of earlier rounds of PR 108.
- **November 3, 2026, Modal keeps no copy of what Guardian checks, once the old app is stopped.** At David's request (LAP-765), since Guardian now sees each action unredacted. Modal's security page (modal.com/docs/guide/security, Data retention) lists inputs and outputs of Functions, Web Functions included, as kept "Up to 7 days, then deleted", and request and response payloads of a Server as "Not stored" and "proxied directly to your container". It lists no workspace or app setting that shortens the 7 days. So Guardian is now one Modal Server (`@app.server`, named `check`) on the GPU in a new app, `mano-guardian-server`, running vLLM in-process behind the same single POST route, the same bearer token and Modal-Key/Modal-Secret proxy auth, and the same request and response bodies. There is no CPU web function in front and no `.remote` or `.spawn` call carrying an action, and the heartbeat Dict is gone. What the Server does not store: the action Engine sends, the verdict, and the model's review, by Modal or by our code (no prints, no log lines, uvicorn's access log off). What is stored: app and container logs of startup and errors, which name what failed and never the text (1 day on Starter, 30 on Team); Modal's app and container metadata; the model weights volume, shared by every Guardian app. The proxy terminates TLS and sees each request in flight. The old app, `mano-guardian`, is a web Function, and merging does not touch it, so Engine keeps working on its old URL. Until it is stopped, Modal keeps every input it receives for up to 7 days each, so the no-copy claim holds only after the steps below. Its old `mano-guardian-state` Dict held only a heartbeat time and is deleted by Modal 7 days after its last write. The order David follows: (1) merge, which deploys `mano-guardian-server` and prints its URL, `<workspace>--mano-guardian-server-check.us-east.modal.direct` (a Server answers on `modal.direct`; the same label on `modal.run` returns 404); (2) copy that URL into the `MANO_GUARDIAN_URL` GitHub secret and its Infisical copy, and into the agent's and the quality nightly's copies; (3) redeploy Engine; (4) check that Engine's `/health` shows `checker: guardian` and run one live check; (5) run the `guardian-modal` workflow from `main` with target `stop-old`, which stops `mano-guardian`. Inputs it already holds are deleted 7 days after each was received. Behavior changes with the Server: with no container up, Modal's proxy answers 503 at once instead of queueing, and Engine reads that as a cold start ("try again in a few minutes", and warm-up state `starting`). On staging the proxy also answered 503 once for about a second right after the container became ready, so a check that gets 503 retries once after a second when a full check (7 seconds) still fits its 10 second budget; a warm-up never retries. Staging measured cold starts of 186 and 209 seconds and warm checks of 4.4 to 6.3 seconds. The 10-minute warm hold is now the Server's `scaledown_window` of 600 seconds after the last request, and every check already sends a warm-up. The `guardian-modal` workflow's `staging` target deploys any branch to `mano-guardian-staging` to try it live first.
- **November 2, 2026, Mano asks instead of guessing, and the declaration is the person's.** Propel tested general AI agents on real SNAP applications (April 2026): they guessed missing fields, cut a mistyped phone number to 10 digits instead of asking, and ticked the legal attestation themselves. Mano's renewal answers each one. Every value comes from the person's own answers or their profile (where a letter's facts land) and passes the same check either way; one that is missing or does not fit its field becomes one plain question, one at a time, in the form's order of importance: case number, name, date of birth, household size, income, address, phone, then the proof document. A value on file that does not fit is named in the question, never shortened or changed. Text goes to the portal exactly as given, less spaces around it; only dates and amounts are written the way the field stores them. A run of spaces inside a text value becomes one space when the value is taken, because a double space carries no meaning on a form and the text line shows it as one, so the person sees and approves the single-spaced value that is filed. A new call to fill the renewal from a later turn closes the version already shown before it asks anything, so a YES to that version never files a value the person has since corrected. A call that ends without holding a new version (it asks a question instead) also closes the drafts its own turn made, so the reply never carries a question for a version the person corrected in the same message. While a renewal the person already approved has no outcome yet (its filing timed out and the portal cannot be asked), Mano prepares nothing new and says it is still checking that one. A new request withdraws it in Engine: it is still reconciled first, and a filing that went through still answers its receipt, but once the portal confirms it never arrived, a YES or the recovery sweep ends it and never sends the old values. Ending it is a compare-and-set on the attempt's version, read before the lookup, so a retry or filing that ran in between keeps its outcome and a receipt always wins; settling it also clears the question's pointer, so the correction is prepared without another YES. A corrected draft from the same turn that Engine does not confirm closed, after one retry, is never shown by the turn's reply, and Mano says plainly that nothing will be sent. The renewal ends with the office's declaration under penalty of perjury. The draft freezes it word for word, the approval question shows it and says only the person can make it, and their YES makes it. Engine attaches who answered from the approval itself, never from caller input, and the sandbox executor files only with that answer from the applicant, sending the declaration as `{ statement, madeBy: "applicant" }`; the portal refuses any filing without it, and its browser pages have nothing to tick. Because a policy that allows submits without a YES has no answer to carry, such a policy can no longer file a renewal to the sandbox portal. The renewal also asks for the case number, read from the person's one food benefits case on file.
